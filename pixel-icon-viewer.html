THE ART — flat, 24×24

The whole icon is this and nothing else. The outline is generated from the silhouette, so it is exactly one pixel on every icon in the catalogue.

IN THE COLUMN

Flat art, three pixels of thickness behind it, unlit. The only thing that says it is solid is the darker colour on the side walls as it swings past. The totem never free-spins an icon — it yaws it to face the player — so this is the whole range of angles it is ever seen at.
